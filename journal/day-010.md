# Day 010 - Rebase vs Merge

## Lesson - git rebase, resolving rebase conflicts

What happened:

- Hands-on practice today, learned the difference between merge and rebase, and when it's safe vs. not safe to use rebase.

Mistake made along the way (or things I found confusing):

- The way `git log --graph --all` laid out the commits and showed diverged history or conflicts was confusing at first, but then I noticed it was just showing how the history was and where it diverged.
- When resolving a conflict I didn't remove the marker text and leftover commit ref text properly - just a mistake, easy to fix, and we got to use `git commit --amend` again.

Key takeaway:

- `merge` preserves history and `rebase` rewrites history for a cleaner one. NEVER rebase shared history unless it was explicitly coordinated.
